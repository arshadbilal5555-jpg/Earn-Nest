EarnNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

EarnNest is a rewards platform that lets users create an account, complete eligible tasks or surveys, earn rewards, request withdrawals, use referrals and contact support.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as name, email address and optional phone number.
  • Authentication and security information such as password hashes, verification records, session tokens, login-attempt and fraud-prevention records.
  • Rewards and financial information such as wallet balances, transactions, withdrawal requests and payment/wallet details supplied for a withdrawal.
  • Verification/KYC information that you voluntarily submit when KYC is required for your account or withdrawal.
  • Support messages, referral information and task/survey completion records.
  • Technical information such as IP address and user-agent information used for security and fraud prevention.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify users, operate rewards and withdrawals, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, process eligible provider callbacks, maintain audit/security records, and comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when needed to operate the service, such as transactional email, authentication, task/survey providers, payout providers, hosting/database providers, or when required by law. Third-party providers may process data under their own privacy terms.

Payments and KYC

If you request a withdrawal, we process the payment information needed for the selected payout method. Where KYC is required, submitted identity information is used for verification, fraud prevention and legal/compliance purposes.

Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. Some security, fraud, transaction or compliance records may be retained after account deletion where required to protect the service or comply with law; such retention will be limited to the necessary information.

Security

Passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes. We use authentication controls, request limits and security logging. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and account deletion

You can request deletion of your EarnNest account and associated personal data from inside the app through Profile → Account deletion, or through the external Account Deletion Request page. We may need to verify the request. Where legitimate legal, fraud-prevention or transaction-retention obligations apply, the minimum necessary information may be retained as described above.

Children

EarnNest is not intended for children. Do not create an account if you are not legally permitted to use the service in your jurisdiction.

Contact

Privacy and deletion requests: support@earnnest.app.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The updated date above will be changed when revisions are published.
