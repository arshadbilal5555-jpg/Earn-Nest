EarnNest Terms & Earning Rules

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Service

EarnNest provides access to eligible rewards activities. Availability, eligibility and rewards can change and are subject to provider rules.

Rewards

Rewards are issued only for genuine, verified activity. EarnNest does not guarantee a fixed income or a particular number of tasks, surveys or rewards.

Referrals

Referral rewards require genuine qualifying activity. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, fraud and artificial activity are not eligible.

Withdrawals

Withdrawals are subject to the configured minimum, verification requirements, fraud checks, available payout methods and manual review. Processing times can vary.

Prohibited activity

Do not use bots, automation, fake identities, duplicate accounts, fraudulent proofs, manipulated provider callbacks or other methods intended to obtain rewards improperly.

Play Store ratings

EarnNest does not reward or require users to submit ratings or reviews. Any feedback should be genuine and voluntary.

Account deletion

You may request deletion through the app or the external deletion page.
